Special Orders outside combat are inspiration for plausible actions, not a currency or automatic bonus. Use [[rules/roleplay|ordinary RP tests]].

## Distracting a patrol
A character with suitable equipment creates a false maintenance alarm. The GM decides whether the patrol is likely to respond or whether an INT or FEL test is needed. [[special-orders/ghost-signal|Ghost Signal]] does not guarantee a reaction outside combat.

## Forcing a hatch
A Brute attempts to force a jammed hatch. Use STR if the outcome is uncertain and failure matters. [[special-orders/make-a-door|Make a Door]] does not automatically grant its combat bonus.

## Working through an AI
An attached AI accesses an available terminal while its host works on a physical task. Establish access and any required INT test. [[special-orders/parallel-processing|Parallel Processing]] does not bypass security or supply missing interfaces.

## Medical expertise
A Marine with INT 0 and Field Medicine rolls 1, 3, 6 against difficulty 2. The failed test has one success. Its [[rules/skills|Skill]] allows the 1 to be rerolled; a 5 brings the total to two successes. [[rules/treatment|Treatment supplies and limits]] still apply.

## A fortunate discovery
AW determines whether you find a concealed supply case. If its contents have not been established, LCK might determine whether it contains useful ammunition. Resolve that circumstance once; the whole party does not each roll Luck.

## Flood resolve
When a situation calls for WIL, a Flood form succeeds through [[keywords/collective-will|Collective Will]]. This does not prevent Pinning or [[keywords/stun|Stun]], which do not require WIL checks.
