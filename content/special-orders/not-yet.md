---
title: "Not Yet"
tags: ["special-orders", "shared-special-orders"]
---

**Restriction:** None.

**When:** Damage would incapacitate you without killing you outright.

**Effect:** Resolve the damage, then perform one immediate short action before becoming incapacitated. For that action only, ignore the incapacitation caused by this damage.

You cannot heal yourself, restore shields, or remove the cause of your incapacitation. Other conditions still apply. This counts toward your out-of-activation allowance if used outside your activation.

See [[rules/special-orders|Special Orders Rules]].
