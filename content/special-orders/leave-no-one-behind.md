---
title: "Leave No One Behind"
tags: ["special-orders", "shared-special-orders"]
---

*The mission isn’t over while someone is still out there.*

**Restriction:** None; requires the physical ability to carry or drag the ally.

**When:** During your activation, you are within interaction range of an incapacitated ally.

**Effect:** Move up to 4" while carrying or dragging them. Place them beside you in a legal position. Neither character may enter engagement. This does not heal or stabilize the casualty.

See [[rules/special-orders|Special Orders Rules]].
