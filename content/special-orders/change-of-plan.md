---
title: "Change of Plan"
tags: ["special-orders", "shared-special-orders"]
---

**Restriction:** None.

**When:** During your activation, the squad acts on a newly discovered threat, lost objective, or information that changes the mission.

**Effect:** You and one willing ally who can hear you may each make an Advance move toward cover or an extraction route. Neither may enter engagement.

See [[rules/special-orders|Special Orders Rules]].
