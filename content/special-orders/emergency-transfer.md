---
title: "Emergency Transfer"
tags: ["special-orders", "artificial-intelligences"]
---

*“Host integrity critical. Moving.”*

**Restriction:** AIs with [[keywords/digital|Digital]], attached to a friendly host.

**When:** Your host would be incapacitated or destroyed.

**Effect:** Before removing the host or resolving consequences for its attached AI, transfer to a willing, conscious friendly character within 4" that has [[keywords/neural-interface|Neural Interface]] or [[keywords/construct|Construct]] and no attached [[keywords/digital|Digital]] model.

This order explicitly permits the transfer at this trigger. It restores nothing and grants no action or activation. It counts toward the AI’s out-of-activation allowance.

Resolve the original attack and any area effects normally. The new host receives no protection from them. Without an eligible destination, you cannot use this order.

See [[rules/special-orders|Special Orders Rules]].
