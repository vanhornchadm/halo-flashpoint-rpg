---
title: "Crashing Charge"
tags: ["special-orders", "brutes-jiralhanae"]
---

*The warriors in your path are obstacles, not your destination.*

**Restriction:** Brutes.

**When:** While unengaged, you declare an Advance or Sprint that will engage a chosen enemy.

**Effect:** During that movement, you may pass through other enemies’ positions and engagement ranges without triggering Assaults against them.

You cannot end overlapping a model. Terrain blocks movement normally. Resolve the normal Assault against your declared target when you reach it.

See [[rules/special-orders|Special Orders Rules]].
