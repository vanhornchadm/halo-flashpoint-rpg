---
title: "Relentless Pursuit"
tags: ["special-orders", "flood"]
---

*Distance is only a temporary reprieve.*

**Restriction:** Flood.

**When:** An enemy begins voluntary movement within 4" of you and finishes farther away.

**Effect:** After that movement and any Break Away test resolve, move up to 4" toward the enemy, finishing closer. You may enter engagement without gaining a free Assault.

You cannot use this while Pinned or engaged with a different enemy. A failed Break Away producing no movement does not trigger it.

See [[rules/special-orders|Special Orders Rules]].
