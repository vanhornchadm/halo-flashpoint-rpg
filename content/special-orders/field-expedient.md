---
title: "Field Expedient"
tags: ["special-orders", "shared-special-orders"]
---

*“That’s not what it’s for.” “It is now.”*

**Restriction:** None; requires the ability to operate the equipment used.

**When:** You attempt a technical or environmental task using equipment in an improvised way.

**Effect:** Attempt the task without the usual specialist tool if you describe a plausible method. Make the normal test.

Before you spend the order, the GM states any equipment consumption or additional risk. This cannot bypass passwords, missing information, or physical impossibility.

See [[rules/special-orders|Special Orders Rules]].
