*Two signatures vanish. Two signatures emerge.*

**Restriction:** Prometheans with [[keywords/teleport|Teleport]].

**When:** Before your normal [[keywords/teleport|Teleport]] action, choose a willing Promethean ally within 4" and LOS. Neither character may be engaged.

**Effect:** Carry that ally through the teleport, placing it legally within 4" of your destination and outside enemy engagement.

Your [[keywords/teleport|Teleport]] retains its normal action cost and rules. The ally gains no activation; its relocation counts toward its out-of-activation allowance.

See [[rules/special-orders|Special Orders Rules]].
