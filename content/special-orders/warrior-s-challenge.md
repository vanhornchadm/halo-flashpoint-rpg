*“Let your skill answer mine.”*

**Restriction:** Elites.

**When:** You declare an Assault against an enemy that is not engaged with an ally.

**Effect:** During that Assault, neither combatant receives dice for nearby friendly models, including [[keywords/companion|Companion]] or [[keywords/horde|Horde]] bonuses. Other modifiers apply normally.

The enemy retains its normal defensive choices.

See [[rules/special-orders|Special Orders Rules]].
