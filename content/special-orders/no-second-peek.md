*A violet flash. A falling body. Everyone else learns to keep their head down.*

**Restriction:** Jackals (Kig-yar) equipped with a ranged weapon with [[keywords/sniper-scope|Sniper Scope]].

**When:** During an enemy's activation, that enemy moves voluntarily within your weapon's range and LOS, or declares a Shoot action while within your weapon's range and LOS. You must be unpinned and unengaged.

For movement, declare at a point along the enemy's actual path and pause it there, before it moves farther. For shooting, declare after weapons and targets are chosen but before any attack rolls.

**Effect:** Make one free **long scoped Shoot action** against that enemy with one equipped Sniper Scope weapon. Gain the scope's normal **+2 dice** and **Headshots on 7 or 8**. Resolve range, cover, shields, armor, AP, and damage reduction normally, using the enemy's position when you interrupt.

Apply the weapon's normal damage and effects.

Resolve the shot and all its effects before continuing the interrupted action. An incapacitated or killed enemy cannot continue. Otherwise, resume only if the enemy can still legally perform the action; the order itself does not cancel it or change its declared targets.

This grants only one weapon attack and counts as your out-of-activation action from Special Orders for the round. It does not require you to have saved actions or remained stationary, and it may be used even if you have already activated. Normal ammunition requirements and the shared Command cost and limits still apply.

See [[rules/special-orders|Special Orders Rules]].
