tags: ["special-orders", "spartans"]

*Your visor turns a battlefield silhouette into actionable information.*

**Restriction:** Spartans.

**When:** At the start of your activation, choose an enemy you can see.

**Effect:** The GM reveals one of the following, chosen by you: its current wounds and shields; its armor and defensive keywords; or the profile of one visible weapon.

This does not reveal plans or concealed equipment.

See [[rules/special-orders|Special Orders Rules]].
