*“I’ve calculated their likely response. Sending you the useful part.”*

**Restriction:** AIs with [[keywords/digital|Digital]], attached to a willing friendly host.

**When:** Before your host makes a Ranged, Fight, or Survive test that permits modifiers.

**Effect:** Reserve one available Dice Symbol Command result in addition to paying for this order.

After the host’s initial roll, but before resolving Headshots or comparing final successes, decide whether to spend the reserved result. If spent, roll one additional die and include it in the test; resolve any Headshot it generates normally.

If declined, return the reserved result to the pool. This does not reroll dice or refund the order’s cost.

See [[rules/special-orders|Special Orders Rules]].
