---
title: "Target Acquired"
tags: ["special-orders", "marines"]
---

*“Marked target. Concentrate your fire.”*

**Restriction:** UNSC Marines.

**When:** You score at least 1 success on a direct Shoot test, whether or not it causes damage.

**Effect:** Mark the target over comms. Until the round ends, the next two eligible direct ranged attacks by allies who can hear you against that target each gain 1 die.

This does not stack with another Target Acquired bonus or apply to area attacks or tests prohibiting modifiers.

See [[rules/special-orders|Special Orders Rules]].
