---
title: "Ghost Signal"
tags: ["special-orders", "shared-special-orders"]
---

*One false contact. One distracted patrol. One way through.*

**Restriction:** None; requires suitable deception equipment or access to a compromised system.

**When:** Before you are detected, you create a false transmission, hologram, or sensor contact.

**Effect:** Place a plausible false contact within the equipment’s range. One unaware guard or patrol capable of perceiving it turns its attention toward it and begins investigating.

The GM determines its route and precautions. Clear contradictory evidence ends the deception. It cannot compel movement into obvious danger or automatically conceal you.

See [[rules/special-orders|Special Orders Rules]].
