---
title: "Borrowed Flesh"
tags: ["special-orders", "flood"]
---

*Nothing on this battlefield needs to go to waste.*

**Restriction:** Flood forms capable of consuming biomass.

**When:** During your activation, you are within interaction range of an unconsumed organic corpse. **You may use this order while Pinned, before taking your normal actions.**

**Effect:** Consume the corpse to recover 1 wound or remove Pinning. If you choose healing while Pinned, you remain Pinned and must follow the normal Stand Up requirement.

The corpse is destroyed and cannot provide further healing or serve as an [[keywords/infection|Infection]] host. This grants no memories, abilities, or new Flood forms.

See [[rules/special-orders|Special Orders Rules]].
