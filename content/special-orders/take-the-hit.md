---
title: "Take the Hit"
tags: ["special-orders", "shared-special-orders"]
---

*You see the muzzle turn before your teammate does.*

**Restriction:** None; requires a physical body capable of intercepting the attack.

**When:** An ally within 4" is targeted by a direct ranged attack, before any rolls or effects resolve.

**Effect:** If unpinned and unengaged, move up to 4" between the attacker and your ally and become the target. Determine range, LOS, modifiers, and defenses using your new position and profile.

You cannot enter engagement. The attacker must retain a legal attack against you. This cannot intercept an area attack.

See [[rules/special-orders|Special Orders Rules]].
