---
title: "Sensor Echo"
tags: ["special-orders", "artificial-intelligences"]
---

*“They’re tracking you. Let’s give them something else to track.”*

**Restriction:** AIs with [[keywords/digital|Digital]], attached to a willing friendly host, with established access to the attacker’s sensor or targeting network.

**When:** An enemy using that network targets your host with a direct Shoot attack, before rolls or effects resolve.

**Effect:** The attacker chooses: resolve the attack with 1 fewer die, to a minimum of 1, or cancel it without spending the declared action.

If canceled, the attacker cannot target your host again during that activation, but may otherwise act normally.

This cannot affect area attacks, tests prohibiting modifiers, or attacks relying solely on unaided sight.

See [[rules/special-orders|Special Orders Rules]].
