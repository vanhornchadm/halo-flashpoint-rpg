---
title: "Strike from Concealment"
tags: ["special-orders", "elites-sangheili"]
---

*The first warning is the attack itself.*

**Restriction:** Elites.

**When:** You declare a Shoot or Assault against an enemy unaware of your location.

**Effect:** Add 1 die to your attack test. Afterward, if unengaged, you may move up to 4" without entering engagement.

[[keywords/active-camouflage|Active Camouflage]] alone does not establish unawareness. The movement does not automatically conceal you. This order cannot modify tests that prohibit modifiers.

See [[rules/special-orders|Special Orders Rules]].
