title: "Hostile Takeover"
tags: ["special-orders", "artificial-intelligences"]

*Your defenses are failing. Your body is mine now.*

**Restriction:** AIs with [[keywords/digital|Digital]].

**When:** During your activation, you spend one short action to target an enemy [[keywords/construct|Construct]] within 8" and LOS of your host or storage device. You may use this order while unattached or engaged.

Alternatively, when your [[keywords/neural-interface|Neural Interface]] host would make a CC attack against an eligible target, it may forgo that attack to provide this opportunity. You must still spend your short action; this is not a free AI action.

**Effect:** The target must have no attached [[keywords/digital|Digital]] character. It makes a 4-dice Survive test (1), losing one die for each wound already suffered, to a minimum of one die. Depleted shields do not count as wounds. No other dice modifiers apply.

If your host provided the opportunity, the target loses one additional Survive die, to a minimum of one.

On failure, detach from your current location and attach to the target, controlling its movement and actions. On success, nothing happens. Headshots apply normally.

Possession restores nothing and grants no additional activation or actions. Use the possessed host's remaining activation allowance.

Possession ends when you detach, transfer elsewhere, the host is destroyed, or an applicable purge effect succeeds. Losing LOS alone does not end it.

Voluntary detachment costs a short action and requires an accessible legal destination.

See [[rules/special-orders|Special Orders Rules]].
