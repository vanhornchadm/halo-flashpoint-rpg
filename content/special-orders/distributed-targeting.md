---
title: "Distributed Targeting"
tags: ["special-orders", "prometheans"]
---

**Restriction:** Prometheans.

**When:** You declare a direct Shoot attack against an enemy visible to another friendly Promethean within 8" of you.

**Effect:** Use that ally’s LOS to identify the target. Measure range and determine cover from your own position.

A physically unobstructed projectile path must exist from your weapon to the target. You cannot gain Clear Shot from the ally’s view. This does not apply to area attacks.

See [[rules/special-orders|Special Orders Rules]].
