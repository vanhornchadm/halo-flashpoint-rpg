---
title: "Parallel Processing"
tags: ["special-orders", "artificial-intelligences"]
---

*“You handle the firefight. I’ll get the door.”*

**Restriction:** AIs with [[keywords/digital|Digital]], attached to a willing friendly host with [[keywords/neural-interface|Neural Interface]] or [[keywords/construct|Construct]].

**When:** During your host’s activation, it is within interaction range of an electronic mission objective you can access.

**Effect:** Despite [[keywords/digital|Digital]]’s normal participation limits, perform one free short electronic interaction with that objective, using your own applicable stats.

Normal tests and access requirements apply. The interaction cannot attack, use [[keywords/intrusion-protocol|Intrusion Protocol]] or [[special-orders/hostile-takeover|Hostile Takeover]], control another character, or grant additional actions.

It consumes none of the host’s actions and grants the AI no activation. It counts toward the AI’s out-of-activation allowance.

See [[rules/special-orders|Special Orders Rules]].
