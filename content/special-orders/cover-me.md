---
title: "Cover Me"
tags: ["special-orders", "shared-special-orders"]
---

**Restriction:** None.

**When:** You cause at least 1 wound with a Shoot action.

**Effect:** A willing ally who can see or hear you may immediately make an Advance move or perform one free short interaction with a mission objective.

Normal tests and interaction requirements apply. The granted action cannot deal damage or bring the ally into engagement.

See [[rules/special-orders|Special Orders Rules]].
