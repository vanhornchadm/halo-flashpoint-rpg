---
title: "Snap Shot"
tags: ["special-orders", "spartans"]
---

*The enemy closes the distance. Your sights are already there.*

**Restriction:** Spartans.

**When:** An enemy finishes an Advance or Sprint that engages you and grants a free Assault against you. Declare after they choose you as the target, before resolving the Assault. You cannot be Pinned or engaged with another enemy.

**Effect:** Make one free short Shoot action against the attacker with one equipped ranged weapon, despite engagement with that enemy.

Ignore Clear Shot, High Ground, and Friendly [[keywords/fire|Fire]] modifiers. Other weapon rules apply. [[keywords/fast-transition|Fast Transition]] cannot add a second weapon attack.

If the attacker is Pinned, incapacitated, killed, or moved out of engagement, its free Assault does not occur.

See [[rules/special-orders|Special Orders Rules]].
