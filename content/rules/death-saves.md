title: "Spartan Down — Death Saves"

These campaign rules apply to living player characters and designated important NPCs, regardless of species. Constructs, Flood, [[keywords/infection|infection deadlines]], and explicitly unsurvivable events follow their own rules.

## Downed

When a living player character reaches **0 HP**, they become **Downed**. Leave their model in place. They cannot act and begin with no failed death saves.

## Making a Death Save

Starting at the end of the **following round**, and at the end of each round thereafter while unstable, roll **3d8 against their SV**, with exploding 8s as normal.

| Successes | Result |
|---|---|
| **0–1** | **Deteriorating:** Mark one failed death save. |
| **2** | **Holding on:** No change. |
| **3+** | **Stable:** Stop making death saves. You remain unconscious at 0 HP. |

**Three failed death saves mean death.** Failures remain until the casualty is stabilized. Stabilization, whether from a death save or medical aid, clears failures.

Death saves receive no cover, equipment, keyword, or Command Dice bonuses or rerolls unless a rule explicitly affects death saves. Armor and shields do not modify the death save itself.

## Medical Aid

Successful stabilization immediately stops death saves and clears failures. Use the existing [[rules/treatment|Medical Treatment and Repair]] actions and supply requirements.

A stabilized casualty remains unconscious at 0 HP. Ordinary medical treatment cannot return them to combat during the current encounter. Recovery outside combat follows the treatment rules.

## Further Harm

Each separate damaging attack or effect that causes HP loss after normal defenses adds **one failed death save**, regardless of damage amount. If stable, the casualty becomes unstable again with one failure.

Stabilization does not prevent new damage, remove infection, extinguish fire, or protect against environmental hazards.
