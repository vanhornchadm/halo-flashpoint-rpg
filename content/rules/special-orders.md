*Training wins engagements. Sometimes survival demands something extraordinary.*

Special Orders represent moments of exceptional coordination, ingenuity, determination, or instinct. Each order has a restriction, a trigger, and an effect.

## The Shared Command Pool

During combat, the player characters and their accompanying allies count as one fireteam and share one Command pool.

At the beginning of each round:

1. Roll **2 Command Dice**, plus additional dice granted by eligible friendly characters with **[[keywords/tactician|Tactician (n)]]**.
2. Apply [[keywords/tactician|Tactician]] rerolls normally.
3. Place the results where everyone can see them.

[[keywords/tactician|Tactician]] bonuses from multiple eligible characters add together. A character must be present, conscious, and able to participate to contribute these benefits.

Players agree how to spend the shared results. Each player retains control of their own character; an order cannot compel an unwilling ally to act.

Discard spent dice. At the end of the round, discard unspent results except those retained through [[keywords/tactician|Tactician]]. Retained results count toward the next round’s total pool size; they do not increase it.

The GM manages enemy Command pools separately. Enemies do not receive additional pools simply because they belong to different species.

## Access and Cost

A character may use any Special Order for which they meet the listed restriction and trigger, unless the campaign uses a separate rule for learning orders.

Using an order costs **one Special Order Command Die result**, or one token that explicitly permits a Special Order. Check eligibility and declare any required choices before paying. A failed test does not refund the cost.

Restrictions apply to the user. Benefiting allies may belong to any species or faction unless the order says otherwise.

Spartan, Marine, and ODST refer to designated character types. Being human alone does not qualify a character for all three.

## Combat Limits

- **Each character may use one Special Order per round.**
- **Each side may use one Special Order during any single activation**, whether friendly or enemy.
- **Each character may receive no more than one out-of-activation action or movement from Special Orders per round.**
- Actions or movements granted by a Special Order cannot trigger another Special Order.

Receiving a benefit does not use the recipient’s personal order allowance unless they are also the user. A benefit that grants no action or movement does not consume the recipient’s out-of-activation allowance.

These limits reset at the beginning of the next round.

## Actions, Movement, and Timing

Resolve an order at its stated trigger, before continuing the interrupted sequence. Resolve attacks and their consequences completely unless an order explicitly interrupts an earlier step.

A free action does not consume a normal action and may repeat an action already performed. It does not grant another activation or remove an activation marker.

Unless stated otherwise, movement granted by an order:
- Is voluntary movement of up the character's Advance Speed stat.
- Follows normal terrain and Break Away rules.
- Cannot be taken by a character unable to move.
- Does not grant a free Assault when entering engagement.

Use the campaign’s normal engagement and interaction distances. A **Fight test** is a roll made during an **Assault action**, not a separate action.

Special Orders do not override equipment, keyword, LOS, or action restrictions unless explicitly stated.

## Ordinary Command Results

Advance, Shoot, Assault, Dice Symbol, and +1 Model remain ordinary Command results.

A character may use only **one of Advance, Shoot, or Assault** during its activation. A Special Order is separate from that limit.

The ordinary Advance Command result grants up to the first value in the character's SPD stat. Shoot and Assault follow their normal rules.

**+1 Model replaces On My Mark.** After a friendly activation, the party may spend +1 Model to activate a second character who has not activated that round. This cannot be used on the party’s first turn of the round. Finish the first activation before beginning the second; actions cannot be interleaved. No more than two characters may activate on that party turn.

The second activation remains that character’s normal activation for the round. The result’s normal end-phase initiative option applies only if the campaign retains Flashpoint’s alternating-side initiative system.

## Conditions and Physical Capability

A Pinned character cannot use an order unless it explicitly permits use while Pinned.

Incapacitated characters cannot use orders unless the trigger explicitly allows use as they become incapacitated. [[keywords/stun|Stun]] and other action prohibitions still apply unless specifically overridden.

“No species or faction restriction” does not remove physical limitations. A [[keywords/digital|Digital]] character cannot intercept a projectile or carry a casualty. [[keywords/beast|Beast]] does not gain permission to operate equipment.

## Artificial Intelligences

An AI is a separate character for its personal once-per-round allowance, even while attached to a host. It spends from the party’s shared pool.

An AI and its host may not both use an order during the same activation because the party’s one-order-per-activation limit still applies.

Attachment does not grant the AI an additional activation. [[keywords/digital|Digital]], [[keywords/neural-interface|Neural Interface]], and [[keywords/intrusion-protocol|Intrusion Protocol]] continue to govern its normal participation. An AI order grants only the specific exception stated in its effect.

Any movement of a host carries its attached AI normally; this does not separately consume the AI’s out-of-activation movement allowance.



## Special Orders Outside Combat

Outside combat, characters may attempt suitable activities described by their Special Orders without spending Command Dice or tracking uses.

Resolve these attempts through ordinary roleplay: straightforward actions succeed; uncertain actions with meaningful consequences require an appropriate test. Species, equipment, access, and physical limitations still apply.

The order’s combat benefits—free actions, additional movement, bonus dice, automatic information, or guaranteed enemy behavior—do not automatically apply outside combat. The GM determines the outcome from the character’s approach and the situation.

Characters do not need a matching Special Order to attempt something they could ordinarily do.
