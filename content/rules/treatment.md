*Expertise keeps people and machines operational. Supplies make the work possible.*

These rules replace unrestricted healing from [[keywords/medic|Medic]] and split ordinary [[keywords/repair|Repair]] from Knight reconstruction.

They require no rest schedule or new recovery currency. Medical supplies and repair parts are expendable equipment.

## Supplies

One **medical supply use** supports one treatment attempt. One **repair supply use** supports one repair attempt.

A kit may contain several uses. Record them like ammunition or other expendable equipment. The GM establishes available quantities when supplies are obtained.

A supply use is consumed when treatment begins, whether a required test succeeds or fails. Tools are reusable unless an effect damages or consumes them.

Promethean reconstruction charges count as repair supplies. Supplies must be compatible with the target.

## Medical Treatment During Combat

### With Medic

Once during your activation, while within 4" and LOS of an eligible target, spend one medical supply use and perform a **free Auxiliary action**.

Choose one:

- Restore **1 HP** to a conscious living target.
- Stabilize a living target at zero HP.
- Remove an Infested marker instead of healing, destroying the attached infection.

You must have suitable medical equipment and physical access to the patient. You may treat yourself if conscious and physically capable.

Each target may benefit from only **one medical treatment per round**, regardless of the number of medics.

### Without Medic

A character with suitable tools and supplies may attempt the same treatment as a **long action**, making a standard **INT test requiring 2 successes**.

A relevant Skill, such as Field Medicine, applies normally. Success produces the chosen effect; failure consumes the supplies without producing it.

The same once-per-target-per-round limit applies.

### Stabilizing a Casualty

A stabilized character remains at zero HP and cannot act. Stabilization prevents deterioration caused solely by the existing injury.

For eligible casualties, stabilization stops [[rules/death-saves|death saves]] and clears all failed death saves. See **[[rules/death-saves|Spartan Down]]** for becoming Downed, death save timing, and the effects of further damage.

It does not stop new damage, remove infection, extinguish fire, or protect against environmental hazards. New damage can put the casualty in danger again.

Ordinary medical treatment cannot return a zero-HP character to combat during the current encounter.

## Medical Treatment Outside Combat

With appropriate supplies, tools, access, and time, treatment of ordinary wounds succeeds without a roll.

Each medical supply use restores **1 HP**, up to the target’s maximum. The GM calls for an INT test only when complications make the outcome uncertain—for example, unfamiliar anatomy, damaged equipment, or dangerous conditions.

A relevant medical Skill grants its normal rerolls. [[keywords/medic|Medic]] establishes combat efficiency; it does not create supplies or bypass unusual complications.

A stabilized, living casualty may be treated outside combat. The first successful treatment restores it to 1 HP and consciousness, provided the injury does not separately prevent this.

Serious injuries, destroyed organs, or other established lasting harm are not erased merely by restoring HP. Their treatment follows the fiction.

Characters may use additional supplies to treat additional missing HP. Repeating a failed complicated procedure still requires the meaningful change demanded by the normal RP rules.

## Construct Repair During Combat

### With Repair

Once during your activation, spend one repair supply use and take a **short Auxiliary action** to restore **1 HP** to another operational friendly [[keywords/construct|Construct]] within 4" and LOS.

You need suitable tools and physical or compatible system access.

Each [[keywords/construct|Construct]] may benefit from this repair only **once per round**, regardless of how many repairers are present.

### Without Repair

A character with suitable tools and parts may attempt the same work as a **long action**, making a standard **INT test requiring 2 successes**.

Relevant technical Skills apply. Failure consumes the parts without restoring HP.

### Repair Limits

Ordinary [[keywords/repair|Repair]]:

- Cannot restore Energy Shields.
- Cannot replenish ammunition or spent equipment.
- Cannot resurrect a destroyed [[keywords/construct|Construct]].
- Does not count as [[keywords/medic|Medic]].
- Does not restore an AI simply because its storage device has been repaired.

## Construct Repair Outside Combat

Routine repairs with compatible parts, tools, access, and time require no roll. Each repair supply use restores **1 HP**.

Unfamiliar systems, severe damage, or hazardous conditions may require an INT test. Relevant Skills apply normally.

Rebuilding a destroyed [[keywords/construct|Construct]] is a separate undertaking requiring sufficient surviving components and appropriate facilities or technology. Ordinary spare parts do not guarantee resurrection.

## Knight Reconstruction

A character with [[keywords/knight-reconstruction|Knight Reconstruction]] and functioning compatible systems may rebuild an eligible destroyed friendly Promethean Knight.

During combat:

1. Be within 4" and LOS of its recoverable remains.
2. Spend one compatible reconstruction charge.
3. Take a **long action**.

The Knight returns in a legal position within 4" of its remains with **2 HP**, no charged Energy Shields, and marked as activated. It cannot activate again that round.

Its shields regenerate normally afterward. Reconstruction restores no ammunition, equipment uses, or other limited abilities.

Each Knight may return through this ability only **once per encounter**. Outside combat, the same capability requires time and a charge; it cannot be repeated on the same destruction.

An enemy within interaction range may use a long action to destroy the remains and prevent reconstruction.

## Interaction with Other Rules

**[[keywords/life-support|Life Support]]:** Retains its separate automatic, one-use effect. [[keywords/medic|Medic]] does not replenish it. A [[equipment/health-pack|Health Pack]] provides this effect or its listed manual full-HP recovery, consuming the same single use. Neither function revives a zero-HP character.

**[[equipment/repair-field|Repair Field]]:** Has three total healing charges and respects the medical treatment or Construct repair limit for each target. It does not stabilize or revive casualties. Remaining charges stay finite outside combat.

**[[keywords/infection|Infection]]:** Healing HP alone never removes an Infested marker. Stabilizing a casualty does not suspend conversion deadlines. Removing the infection is a separate treatment choice.

**Flood:** Conventional medical supplies do not heal Flood. Their recovery follows explicit Flood abilities or appropriate narrative procedures.

**Skills:** Field Medicine and relevant engineering Skills grant RP rerolls on treatment tests. They do not automatically grant the efficient combat actions of [[keywords/medic|Medic]] or [[keywords/repair|Repair]].

**Special Orders:** Orders that move casualties or grant interactions do not create supplies or bypass these treatment limits.
