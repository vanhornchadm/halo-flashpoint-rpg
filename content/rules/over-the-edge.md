Models can be pushed, blasted, or knocked off elevated terrain. Use the published forced-movement and falling rules, summarized below. The near-edge Pinning test and [[keywords/breakfall|Breakfall]] Talent are house additions.

## Forced Movement and Falling

Forced movement can carry a model into a cube with no floor, causing it to fall automatically. There is no Agility test to catch the edge. The model’s controller chooses its placement within the destination cube.

Terrain blocks forced movement normally: solid walls stop movement (use the published Slammed into Walls rule), while suitable gaps permit it. Forced movement cannot use a Hatch. Entering an enemy-occupied cube triggers the normal free Assault, subject to the Pinned rules.

A model falls when it ends a movement action or forced movement in a cube without a floor, or descends more than one level through floorless cubes without climbing down scenery. It falls vertically to a cube with a floor; use the published Scatter rules for partial floors. Measure fall height from the last floor it stood on. A model cannot move upward into a floorless cube in order to fall.

## Falling Damage

Use the published damage procedure without modification. The opposing player rolls Gravity’s **4+ test (X)** using the dice below. The falling model opposes this with a **3-dice Survive test (X)**.

| Fall height | Gravity dice |
|---|---:|
| 1 cube | No test required |
| 2 cubes | 4 |
| 3 cubes | 5 |
| 4+ cubes | 6 |

When a test is required:

- **Gravity wins:** The model becomes Pinned and takes Hits equal to Gravity’s excess successes. Gravity has no Armour Piercing. Resolve the Hits normally.
- **Model wins or ties:** The model becomes Pinned but takes no Hits from the fall.

For a one-cube fall, retain the base game’s treatment of Pinning; this house rule adds no damage test or separate Pinning requirement.

The movement that caused a fall ends after resolving it. A model that is not Pinned may perform other actions if otherwise eligible. [[keywords/flight|Flight]], [[keywords/jump-pack|Jump Pack]], and other existing protections retain their normal effects. MJOLNIR grants no additional fall protection under this rule.

## Knocked Prone Near an Edge — House Rule

When an effect forces a standing model prone or Pins it while its base is within **1" of an unprotected edge**, make a **standard Agility test (2 successes)**.

- **Success:** The model stays where it is, Pinned.
- **Failure:** The model falls over the nearest unprotected edge. Resolve the fall normally.

If an effect both Pins and moves a model, resolve the movement first. If that movement already causes a fall, do not make a near-edge test. Otherwise, test once if the model’s final position meets the conditions above. A model already Pinned does not test merely for being Pinned again. Pinning caused by a fall does not itself trigger another near-edge test.

An intact barrier capable of stopping the model prevents a fall over that edge. Existing immunity to forced movement or Pinning still applies.

The test requires no action. Roll **3d8 plus AGL**, with at least one die. Each **5+** is a success; each **8** generates another die. See [[rules/roleplay|Light Roleplay Rules]].

## Landing on Your Feet

[[keywords/breakfall|Breakfall]] allows an Agility test to ignore Pinning caused by a fall. It does not reduce damage or remove Pinning from the attack or effect that caused the fall.
