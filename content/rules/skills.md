## Skills

Skills are subjects, activities, or areas of expertise your character knows well. Examples include **Field Medicine, Piloting, Covenant History, Demolitions, Tracking, Negotiation,** or **Forerunner Technology**.

### Choosing Skills

Start with **three Skills** based on your character’s background, training, or interests.

Each Skill should cover a recognizable area of expertise. Avoid Skills so broad that they apply to almost everything, such as “Combat,” “Technology,” or “Survival.” Narrow them to something like **Marksmanship, Security Systems,** or **Arctic Survival**.

Agree on each Skill’s scope with the GM. Skills have no ranks or associated points.

### Using a Skill

When you fail a roleplay attribute test involving one of your Skills:

1. Finish resolving the initial roll, including all additional dice generated by 8s.
2. Reroll any dice showing **1** that have not already been rerolled.
3. Keep the new results, even if they are also 1s. Results of **5+** add successes, and **8s** generate additional dice normally.
4. Compare your final successes to the original difficulty.

Use this benefit only once per test. Multiple applicable Skills do not grant additional rerolls. Extra dice generated during the Skill reroll cannot trigger another Skill reroll.

If the failed roll contains no eligible 1s, the Skill grants no reroll.

### Skills and Attributes

A Skill can apply to different attributes when the approach makes sense.

For example, **Demolitions** might apply to:

- **INT:** Disarming an explosive.
- **AW:** Recognizing signs of a concealed charge.
- **AGL:** Carefully placing a charge in a difficult position.

Explain how the Skill helps; the GM determines whether it applies.

Skills do not apply to **Luck**, which represents chance rather than expertise.

### Limits

Skills do not make impossible actions possible or remove requirements for tools, access, or physical capability.

Skill rerolls are part of the original test. Resolve failure consequences only after the rerolls are complete.

These rules apply to **roleplay attribute tests**, including technical or environmental tasks attempted during combat. They do not grant rerolls on **Ranged, Fight, or Survive tests**, which remain governed by combat keywords and abilities.

### Learning Skills

A character may gain a new Skill when meaningful training or experience in the story supports it, with the GM’s agreement. No experience-point costs or training timers are required.
