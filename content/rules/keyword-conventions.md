## Reading Keywords

A keyword describes a specific ability, limitation, or effect. Its location determines its source:

- **Character:** Applies to the character as stated.
- **Weapon:** Applies while using that weapon unless stated otherwise.
- **Equipment:** Applies while the equipment is functioning and used as required.

Keywords are not automatically selectable Talents. Innate abilities may be starting Talents; equipment systems, conditions, and weapon properties are acquired through their own rules.

**Values:** A keyword followed by **(n)** uses the number shown on its profile.

**Stacking:** Different applicable effects combine normally. Multiple copies of the same keyword combine only when it explicitly says they are cumulative.

**Rerolls:** A die may be rerolled only once. Keep its new result.

**Restrictions:** A prohibition on modifiers or rerolls takes precedence over a keyword that normally grants them.

**Tests:** Ranged, Fight, and Survive tests use their normal combat stats. RP attribute tests use the Light Roleplay rules. Skills do not grant rerolls on combat tests.

**Damage:** Suffering one wound means losing one HP. Restoring one HP removes one wound, up to the character’s maximum.

**Actions:** Fight is a test made during an Assault, not a separate action. A free action does not consume the normal action allowance or grant another activation.

**Movement:** Use normal terrain, engagement, and Break Away rules unless a keyword creates an exception. Distances are measured in inches.

**Area effects:** Unless stated otherwise, area effects affect friendly and enemy characters alike. Headshots follow the normal rules unless explicitly prevented.
