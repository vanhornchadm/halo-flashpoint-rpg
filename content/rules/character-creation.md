---
title: "Character Creation"
tags: ["rules", "character-creation"]
---

1. Choose a [[species/index|playable species]] and record its base combat and RP stats.
2. Record its starting Talents. Apply functioning equipment bonuses separately. An improvement to RA, FI, or SV lowers its target number.
3. Choose a background and [[rules/skills|three Skills]]. ODST training requires the ODST background.
4. Agree with the GM on any selections from [[talents/index|Talents]] and [[disadvantages/index|Disadvantages]]. No point budget or automatic trade-in reward has been set.
5. Choose suitable equipment and establish available ammunition, medical supplies, and repair parts.
6. Review [[special-orders/index|Special Orders]] for which your character qualifies. Use the shared pool during combat.
7. Resolve any starred host-dependent stats before play.

Start here for [[rules/roleplay|roleplay tests]], [[rules/treatment|treatment and repair]], and [[rules/profile-notes|profile notation]].
