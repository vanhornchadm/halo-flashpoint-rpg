**Type III · Shoot action**

**Base R16", AP–, [[keywords/emp|EMP]], [[keywords/grenade|Grenade]], [[keywords/implosion|Implosion (3)]], [[keywords/one-use|One-Use]].**

A hand-thrown grenade. **Throwing range: base range + (4" × your current STR), minimum 4".** Follow the Grenade placement test and scatter rules, then resolve its keywords at the final impact point. Area effects can affect allies and enemies.

Resolve EMP before Implosion damage. EMP depletes personal shields in the area; it does not automatically disable vehicles, Constructs, or other electronics.

See [[rules/equipment|Equipment Rules]] and [[equipment/index|Equipment Reference]].
