**Type II · Immediately before your normal Advance or Sprint**

Add **4"** to the distance of one Advance or Sprint made using your normal short or long actions. Declare before beginning that move.

Add the distance once, after determining the selected action's normal movement allowance. This does not multiply through Sprint, grant another action, or affect movement granted by a Special Order. Normal terrain and Break Away rules apply. **[[keywords/one-use|One-Use]].**
