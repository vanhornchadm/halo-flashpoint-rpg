**Type II · Free action during your activation**

Choose one:

- Move up to **12"** toward a visible, suitable anchor, including a change in elevation. Measure the full route, finish in a legal supported position, and follow normal terrain, engagement, and Break Away rules. This movement may be used in addition to Advance or Sprint actions but does not grant a free Assault.
- Retrieve one unattended, portable item or weapon within **4" and LOS** along an unobstructed retrieval path.

This does not automatically pull heavy objects, carry a casualty, or seize equipment held by someone else. Such attempts require a plausible approach and a GM ruling on actions and any [[rules/roleplay|roleplay test]]. **[[keywords/one-use|One-Use]].**
