**Type I · Passive while functioning**

Gain **+1 AR** while carrying this functioning item. Expend it immediately after you next suffer HP loss; its armor bonus applies to the damage resolution that expends it.

Hits absorbed without HP loss do not expend it. Multiple copies do not stack their armor bonus. This version is an armor booster, not a separate directional barrier. **[[keywords/one-use|One-Use]].**
