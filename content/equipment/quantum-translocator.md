**Type II · Immediately before your Advance or Sprint**

Place an anchor marker at your current position before beginning the movement. Once, before the end of that activation, if you are within **8"** of the marker and able to act, return to its position for free.

You do not cross intervening terrain or trigger Break Away. You must have a legal destination; the device cannot place you inside an obstacle or another character. If the return puts you in normal engagement range of an enemy, resolve an Assault, without the **+2 dice for an Advance**.

The return cannot interrupt an unresolved attack or test. Remove the anchor after returning or at the end of the activation, even if you never returned. **[[keywords/one-use|One-Use]].**
