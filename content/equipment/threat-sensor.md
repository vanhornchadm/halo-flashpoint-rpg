**Type II · Short Auxiliary action**

Place a sensor marker on a surface within **12" and LOS**. Until the end of the round, you and your allies know the positions of characters within **4"** of the marker, including through walls, [[keywords/active-camouflage|Active Camouflage]], and [[equipment/shroud-screen|Shroud Screen]].

For attacks against detected characters, ignore the LOS denial and Ranged penalties from Active Camouflage and the LOS blocking from Shroud Screen. Physical terrain still blocks attacks normally and cover still applies. Detection never grants the ability to shoot through solid walls or bypass another action's physical-access requirement.

The sensor reveals positions, not thoughts, allegiance, passwords, or a complete floor plan. **[[keywords/one-use|One-Use]].**
