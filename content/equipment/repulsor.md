**Type II · Short Auxiliary action**

Choose one:

- **Blast:** Choose a direction. Every character within **4" and LOS** inside a **90° arc** facing that way, ally or enemy, moves **8" directly away from you** with no test. Unattended portable items in the arc move the same way.
- **Launch:** Move yourself up to **8"** in a straight line in any direction, including upward, without climbing, and finish in a legal supported position. This is not an Advance or Sprint and grants no free Assault; normal engagement and Break Away rules apply.

A card corner marks the arc. Resolve normal forced-movement consequences, including walls and falls; existing immunity still applies. The blast causes no damage and does not reflect projectiles. **[[keywords/one-use|One-Use]].**
