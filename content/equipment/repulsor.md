**Type II · Short Auxiliary action**

Choose a character within **4" and LOS** and make a standard [[rules/roleplay|INT test requiring 2 successes]]. On success, push it up to **4" directly away from you**, following normal forced-movement consequences. On failure, it does not move.

This version targets characters, not vehicles or terrain, and does not reflect projectiles or grant a reaction. **[[keywords/one-use|One-Use]].**

See [[rules/equipment|Equipment Rules]] and [[equipment/index|Equipment Reference]].
