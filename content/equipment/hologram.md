**Type II · Short Auxiliary action**

Project a visible duplicate of yourself at a legal position within **8" and LOS**. It lasts until the end of the round, cannot attack, interact, provide cover, engage enemies, or block movement, and disappears when hit.

An observer with reason to doubt it may spend a **short Auxiliary action** on a standard [[rules/roleplay|AW test requiring 2 successes]] to identify it. Obvious proof, such as physical contact passing through it, reveals it without a test. For an attack aimed at the duplicate, use a normal Ranged test against your SV in an opposed 3-dice Survive test; the duplicate receives no armor, shields, equipment, or keyword benefits. One resulting Hit dispels it.

The hologram can draw attention, but never forces a player to attack it. Outside combat, it covers one brief distraction or deceptive attempt, with the GM establishing the stakes. **[[keywords/one-use|One-Use]].**
