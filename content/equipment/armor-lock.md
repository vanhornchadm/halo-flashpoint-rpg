**Type II · Long action**

Gain **two temporary shield charges** until the start of your next activation or the encounter ends, whichever comes first. While locked, you cannot move voluntarily, attack, or interact. You still make required defensive tests and can suffer forced movement.

Spend applicable barrier charges first, then Armor Lock charges, then normal personal shields. Lock charges do not regenerate, increase your profile's shield maximum, or change whether your normal shields are fully charged. Effects depleting all personal shields also deplete these charges. Losing the charges does not end the lock early. Any unspent charges expire when the lock ends.

This version provides limited protection, not invulnerability. **[[keywords/one-use|One-Use]].**
