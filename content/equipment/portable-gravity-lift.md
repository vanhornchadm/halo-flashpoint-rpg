**Type II · Short Auxiliary action**

Place a lift marker at your position. Until the encounter ends, any character at the marker may use an **Advance action** to travel up to **8" vertically**, replacing that action's normal movement and ending on a legal supported surface. It does not pass through solid ceilings or other obstacles.

The lift is stationary and may be used by allies or enemies. Normal engagement and Break Away restrictions apply. Carrying a casualty still requires the ability to support and transport them; the lift does not increase carrying capacity.

Outside combat, one deployment lasts for one traversal or evacuation scene, as agreed before deployment. **[[keywords/one-use|One-Use]] deployment.**
