**Type II · Short Auxiliary action**

**[[keywords/thrown|Thrown (12")]].**

Deploy onto a legal supporting surface along an unobstructed path. No placement test is required.

Place a lift marker within Thrown range and LOS, including at your position. Until the encounter ends, any character at the marker may use an **Advance action** to travel up to **8" vertically**, replacing that action's normal movement and ending on a legal supported surface. It does not pass through solid ceilings or other obstacles.

The lift is stationary and may be used by allies or enemies. Normal engagement and Break Away restrictions apply.
