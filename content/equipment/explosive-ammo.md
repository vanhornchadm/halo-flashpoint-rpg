**Type III · Declare before one Ranged test**

Use with a ranged weapon to gain **+1 die and [[keywords/lethal|Lethal (1)]]** for one Ranged test.

Cannot be used with weapons having [[keywords/explosive|Explosive]], [[keywords/grenade|Grenade]], or [[keywords/lunge|Lunge]]. Declare before rolling; a miss still consumes the ammunition. **[[keywords/one-use|One-Use]].**

See [[rules/equipment|Equipment Rules]] and [[equipment/index|Equipment Reference]].
