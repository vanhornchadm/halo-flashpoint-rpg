**Type III · Shoot action**

**[[keywords/thrown|Thrown (12")]], AP1, [[keywords/frag|Frag (4)]], [[keywords/grenade|Grenade]], [[keywords/small-blast|Small Blast]], [[keywords/sticky|Sticky]], [[keywords/one-use|One-Use]].**

A hand-thrown grenade. Follow the Grenade placement test and scatter rules, then resolve its keywords at the final impact point. Area effects can affect allies and enemies.

Sticky depletes the selected character’s personal shields before resolving the remaining effects. It does not move the impact point or create a separate attachment procedure.

See [[rules/equipment|Equipment Rules]] and [[equipment/index|Equipment Reference]].
