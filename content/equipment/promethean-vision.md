**Type II · Short Auxiliary action**

Until the end of your activation, detect the positions of characters within **8" of you**, including through walls, [[keywords/active-camouflage|Active Camouflage]], and [[equipment/shroud-screen|Shroud Screen]]. Only you receive the mechanical benefit.

Against detected characters, ignore camouflage's LOS denial and Ranged penalties and shroud's LOS blocking. Physical terrain and cover still apply; you cannot shoot through solid walls or bypass physical-access requirements. You may describe what you detect to allies, but doing so does not give them these targeting benefits.

Outside combat, one use provides one brief scan. **[[keywords/one-use|One-Use]].**
