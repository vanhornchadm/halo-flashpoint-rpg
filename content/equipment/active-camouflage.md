**Type II · Short Auxiliary action**

Gain [[keywords/active-camouflage|Active Camouflage]] until you Sprint, resolve a Fight test, or Shoot, or until the encounter ends, whichever happens first. End the effect when beginning a Sprint or Shoot, or immediately before resolving a Fight test.

Outside combat, one use covers one infiltration attempt or short scene, as established by the GM before activation. It does not conceal noise, tracks, or other evidence, or automatically make enemies forget your location.

Use the keyword's normal shield-dependent effects. **[[keywords/one-use|One-Use]].**

See [[rules/equipment|Equipment Rules]] and [[equipment/index|Equipment Reference]].
