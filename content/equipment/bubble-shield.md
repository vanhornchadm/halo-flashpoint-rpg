**Type II · Short Auxiliary action**

Place a dome marker at your position with a **4" radius**, lasting until the start of your next activation or the encounter ends, whichever comes first.

Ranged attacks cannot cross the dome boundary **in either direction**. Characters may walk through it normally; attacks wholly inside resolve normally. Explosive effects originating outside cannot affect characters inside, and vice versa. A grenade cannot be thrown or scattered across the boundary; if scatter would cross it, keep the grenade at its original legal target point.

The dome affects allies and enemies alike. It does not stop melee, environmental hazards already inside, or create personal shield charges. **[[keywords/one-use|One-Use]] deployment.**
