**Type I · When first revealed**

Gain the mission benefit attached to this intelligence: for example, an access code, patrol route, objective location, or extraction opportunity. The GM establishes its contents and any access or decoding requirements through the mission and normal [[rules/roleplay|roleplay rules]].

If the encounter uses victory points, the revealing team instead receives **+1 VP**, unless the mission explicitly grants both rewards. Revealing or copying the same intel again does not repeat the reward. Its information can remain useful after the reward is claimed. **[[keywords/one-use|One-Use]] reward.**

See [[rules/equipment|Equipment Rules]] and [[equipment/index|Equipment Reference]].
