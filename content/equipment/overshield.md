**Type II · Short Auxiliary action**

Gain **one temporary shield charge**. Spend applicable barrier charges first, then this charge, then your normal personal [[keywords/energy-shield|Energy Shields]].

The charge does not regenerate or increase your profile's shield maximum. Only one Overshield can be active on a character at a time. It expires when spent, depleted by an effect that depletes all personal shields, or when the encounter ends. It does not change whether your normal shields count as fully charged for camouflage. **[[keywords/one-use|One-Use]].**

See [[rules/equipment|Equipment Rules]] and [[equipment/index|Equipment Reference]].
