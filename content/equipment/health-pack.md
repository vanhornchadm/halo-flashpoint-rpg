**Type I · Passive trigger or short Auxiliary action**

While carrying a compatible pack, gain [[keywords/life-support|Life Support]]: when you suffer wounds but remain above zero HP, immediately restore yourself to full HP and expend the pack.

Alternatively, while conscious and physically able, expend it with a **short Auxiliary action** to restore your own HP to maximum.

Both functions consume the **same single use**. Resolve only one carried pack for a given trigger. Neither function restores shields, revives a zero-HP character, removes infection, or erases established lasting injuries. This is a specific full-healing exception to ordinary [[rules/treatment|medical treatment]], not a reusable medical kit. **[[keywords/one-use|One-Use]].**

See [[rules/equipment|Equipment Rules]] and [[equipment/index|Equipment Reference]].
