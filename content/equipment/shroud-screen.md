**Type II · Short Auxiliary action**

**[[keywords/thrown|Thrown (12")]].**

Deploy onto a legal supporting surface along an unobstructed path. No placement test is required.

Place a marker within Thrown range and LOS, including at your position. Until the end of the round, the **4" radius** area blocks LOS into, out of, and through it. Characters can move through it normally.

Area attacks may still target a legal point under their normal targeting rules; the screen does not grant targeting permission by itself. [[equipment/threat-sensor|Threat Sensor]] can reveal characters through this concealment as specified in its entry. **[[keywords/one-use|One-Use]].**

See [[rules/equipment|Equipment Rules]] and [[equipment/index|Equipment Reference]].
