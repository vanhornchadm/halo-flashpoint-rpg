**Type II · Short Auxiliary action**

**[[keywords/thrown|Thrown (12")]].**

Deploy onto a legal supporting surface along an unobstructed path. No placement test is required.

Place a field marker within Thrown range and LOS, including at your position, with a **4" radius**. It lasts until the encounter ends or the field is destroyed. At each round end, every eligible character in the area restores **1 HP**, never more than once per character that round. Friendly and enemy characters are eligible.

Living recipients must be conscious and above zero HP; Constructs must be operational. Supplies must be compatible with the recipient. Healing counts toward that target's once-per-round medical treatment or Construct repair limit in [[rules/treatment|Medical Treatment and Repair]].

Any character with physical access within 4" of the marker may take a **long action** to destroy the field and remove its marker.
