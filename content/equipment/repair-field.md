**Type II · Short Auxiliary action**

Place a field marker at your position with **three total healing charges** and a **4" radius**. At each round end, spend one charge per eligible character in the area to restore **1 HP** each, never more than once per character that round. If charges are insufficient, the deploying player chooses recipients before spending them. Friendly and enemy characters are eligible.

Living recipients must be conscious and above zero HP; Constructs must be operational. Supplies must be compatible with the recipient. Healing counts toward that target's once-per-round medical treatment or Construct repair limit in [[rules/treatment|Medical Treatment and Repair]]. Conventional supplies do not heal Flood.

The field does not stabilize or revive casualties, restore shields, replenish equipment, remove infection, or erase lasting injuries. It does not move with the user. Remove it when all three charges are spent.

Outside combat, expend its remaining charges at **one charge per HP**, subject to the same eligibility restrictions; no free healing results from waiting. It may be switched off and recovered with a short Auxiliary action at interaction range, retaining only unspent charges. Redeployment costs a short Auxiliary action. **One-Use per healing charge; three charges total.**
