---
title: "Knight Reconstruction"
tags: ["keywords", "character-keywords"]
---

**Requirement:** [[keywords/repair|Repair]] and compatible Promethean Watcher reconstruction systems.

Take a long action within 4" and LOS of a destroyed friendly Promethean Knight’s recoverable remains. Spend one compatible reconstruction charge.

Place the Knight legally within 4" of its remains with **2 HP**, no charged shields, and marked as activated. It cannot activate again that round.

Shields regenerate normally afterward. Reconstruction restores no ammunition, equipment uses, or other limited abilities.

Each Knight may return through this ability only once per encounter. Outside combat, reconstruction still requires suitable remains, time, and a charge.

An enemy with physical access within 4" may take a long action to destroy the remains.
