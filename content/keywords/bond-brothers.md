title: "Bond Brothers"
tags: ["keywords", "character-keywords"]
---

*One falls. The other answers.*

Designate the paired Hunter that shares your bond.

While that partner is taken out of action, you gain **+1 die on tests that permit modifiers**. This does not add to individual die results or modify Command Dice.

The benefit ends if your partner returns to action. Other allies being incapacitated do not trigger it.
