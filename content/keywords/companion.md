---
title: "Companion"
tags: ["keywords", "character-keywords"]
---

*You never truly fight alone.*

During an Assault, you always receive the normal **+1 die** bonus for a nearby friendly character, even when no eligible ally is present.

An actual nearby ally does not increase this particular bonus. Other effects, such as [[keywords/horde|Horde]], apply separately.

If Companion appears on a weapon, it applies only when that weapon is used in the Assault.
