---
title: "Grenade"
tags: ["keywords", "weapon-keywords"]
---

*Arc it over the obstacle and let gravity finish the delivery.*

Take a Shoot action and choose a point within range.

For a **hand-thrown grenade**, maximum range is **the grenade’s base range + (4" × the thrower’s STR)**, to a minimum of **4"**. Use the thrower’s current STR, including functioning equipment bonuses. Negative STR reduces range. Resolve any inherited or unspecified STR before throwing.

For a grenade with a 12" base range, STR −1 gives 8", STR 0 gives 12", STR +1 gives 16", and STR +2 gives 20". A Dynamo Grenade uses its 16" base range instead.

Recalculate range using the new thrower’s STR when a grenade is thrown back, including with [[special-orders/return-to-sender|Return to Sender]]. Weapons that use Grenade placement but are not hand-thrown use their listed range without this STR adjustment.

You need LOS, unless both your position and the target point are visible from directly above, allowing a plausible lob over intervening terrain.

Make a **3-dice Ranged test (1)** with no modifiers or rerolls.

STR changes maximum throwing range only; it does not modify the placement test, scatter distance, or area of effect. Scatter may carry the grenade beyond maximum throwing range.

On success, the grenade lands at the chosen point. On failure, scatter it **4"**.

If this scatter would cross a solid wall or leave the battlefield, it bounces and remains at the original target point.

The placement test is not opposed. Resolve the grenade’s other effects from its final landing point.

A weapon using Grenade placement rules is not automatically a hand-thrown grenade for abilities such as Return to Sender; its equipment description determines that.
