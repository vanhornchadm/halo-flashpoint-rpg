---
title: "Counter Intelligence"
tags: ["keywords", "character-keywords"]
---

*You spot the enemy repositioning and answer in kind.*

If an enemy makes a pre-combat move using [[keywords/scout|Scout]], you may make one free Advance of up to **4"** after all enemy Scout moves have finished, before the first activation.

You must be conscious, unpinned, and able to act. You cannot use this reaction while surprised or restrained, and the movement cannot enter engagement.

This triggers only once per encounter, regardless of how many enemies Scout. Ordinary movement and exploration do not trigger it.
