---
title: "Energy Shield Depleter — ESD (n)"
tags: ["keywords", "weapon-keywords"]
---

When this weapon causes Hits with a Shoot or Assault attack, deplete **n applicable shields** before resolving those Hits.

Use any applicable [[keywords/energy-shield-barrier|Energy Shield Barrier]] first, then personal shields. Resolve the attack’s Hits against the remaining shields and armor normally.

ESD does not require wounds to be inflicted.
