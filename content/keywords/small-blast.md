---
title: "Small Blast"
tags: ["keywords", "weapon-keywords"]
---

After determining the final impact point, center a **3"-diameter circular template** on it.

Every character whose base lies wholly or partially beneath the template is affected by the attack's listed area effects. Area effects affect allies and enemies unless stated otherwise.

If no physical template is available, measure **1.5"** from the center of the impact point to the nearest part of each character's base.

A weapon cannot use Small Blast and [[keywords/large-blast|Large Blast]] at the same time.
