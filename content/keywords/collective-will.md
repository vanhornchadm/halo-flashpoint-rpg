---
title: "Collective Will"
tags: ["keywords", "flood"]
---

**Restriction:** All Flood forms.

Flood automatically succeed on **Will (WIL) checks** without rolling. If a check requires a specific number of successes, they count as having exactly that many.

This applies only when the rules or GM call for a WIL check. It does not prevent Pinning, [[keywords/stun|Stun]], damage, or other effects that do not allow a WIL check.
