---
title: "Implosion (n)"
tags: ["keywords", "weapon-keywords"]
---

After determining the impact point, roll **n dice on a 4+ test (X)**. No modifiers or rerolls apply to this strength roll.

Every character covered by the attack's [[keywords/small-blast|Small Blast]] or [[keywords/large-blast|Large Blast]] template makes a separate **3-dice Survive test (X)**.

If the Implosion roll has more successes, the difference causes Hits. Otherwise, no Hits are caused.

Implosion does not inherently cause Pinning or forced movement.
