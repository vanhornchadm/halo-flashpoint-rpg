*The squad stays connected.*

At the beginning of each combat round, a functioning relay carried by a conscious, participating character generates **one Special Order token** for its side.

An eligible friendly character may spend it instead of a Special Order Command result. Normal order restrictions and limits apply.

Discard the token at the end of the round. Comms Relay generates nothing outside combat.
