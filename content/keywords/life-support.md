---
title: "Life Support"
tags: ["keywords", "equipment-and-integrated-systems"]
---

*The armor fights to keep you alive.*

When you suffer wounds but remain above zero HP, immediately restore yourself to full HP.

This is automatic and **[[keywords/one-use|One-Use]]**. It cannot revive an incapacitated or dead character and restores no shields.

[[keywords/medic|Medic]] and [[keywords/repair|Repair]] do not replenish it.
