---
title: "Energy Shield (n)"
tags: ["keywords", "equipment-and-integrated-systems"]
---

*The first line of defense is invisible.*

You begin with **n charged shields**.

When you take Hits, deplete one shield per Hit canceled. For Hits from a lost opposed test, resolve any remaining Hits against [[keywords/ablative-armour|Ablative Armour]] next, if present. Then resolve remaining Hits against armor and AP and apply other damage rules.

If multiple applicable shield sources protect you, use them before armor. Energy Shield Barriers are depleted before personal shields.

At the beginning of each combat round, restore **one depleted personal shield**, up to the profile’s maximum. Specific equipment restrictions may prevent regeneration.
