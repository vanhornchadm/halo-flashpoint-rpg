---
title: "Breakfall"
tags: ["keywords", "character-keywords", "talents", "house-rules"]
---

After resolving falling damage, if you remain in play and able to act, were not Pinned before the fall, and would become Pinned by the fall, make a **standard Agility test (2 successes)**.

- **Success:** Ignore the Pinning caused by the fall.
- **Failure:** Become Pinned as normal.

This test requires no action. It does not reduce falling damage, prevent the fall, or remove Pinning caused by the attack or effect that forced you off the ledge. Resolve eligibility before applying the fall’s Pinning; that Pinning alone does not prevent this test.

The movement that caused the fall still ends. If you remain eligible to act, you may perform other actions normally.

Roll **3d8 plus AGL**, with at least one die. Each **5+** is a success, and each **8** generates an additional die. See [[rules/roleplay|Light Roleplay Rules]] and [[rules/over-the-edge|Over the Edge]].
