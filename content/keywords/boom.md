---
title: "BOOM! (n)"
tags: ["keywords", "character-keywords"]
---

During your activation, you may take a short Auxiliary action to explode, including while engaged.

Alternatively, during an Assault, you may explode instead of making your Fight or Survive test. Replace the normal opposed test with the explosion; the opponent makes only the Survive test required by that explosion.

You also explode when killed or destroyed.

Resolve [[keywords/frag|Frag (n)]] centered on your position using [[keywords/small-blast|Small Blast]], then remove you from play. Each instance of this keyword causes only one explosion; removal from your own explosion does not trigger it again.
