---
title: "Blast"
tags: ["keywords", "weapon-keywords"]
---

Before resolving damage and other effects, deplete **one personal [[keywords/energy-shield|Energy Shield]]** from every character covered by the attack's [[keywords/small-blast|Small Blast]] or [[keywords/large-blast|Large Blast]] template as well as 4" past the edge of the template, if it has one available.

Resolve the attack’s remaining effects normally.
