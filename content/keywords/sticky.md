title: "Sticky"
tags: ["keywords", "weapon-keywords"]
---

*Once it finds you, the shield is already gone.*

If the [[keywords/grenade|Grenade]] placement test scores at least **2 successes**, choose one character within **4"** of the final impact point and within your LOS.

Deplete all of that character’s personal Energy Shields before resolving the grenade’s remaining effects.

This does not move the impact point or automatically attach the grenade for additional effects.
