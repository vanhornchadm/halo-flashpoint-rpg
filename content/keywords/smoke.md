---
title: "Smoke"
tags: ["keywords", "weapon-keywords"]
---

*The battlefield disappears behind a wall of gray.*

When used with [[keywords/grenade|Grenade]], determine the landing point normally and place a Smoke marker there. The attack causes no damage.

Smoke extends **4" horizontally** from its marker:

- LOS through the smoke is blocked.
- Characters inside cannot draw LOS outside.
- Characters outside cannot draw LOS to characters inside.
- Area weapons may target points inside only if their targeting rules permit it.

A Shoot attack with otherwise legal LOS passing within **4" of the smoke’s edge** suffers **−1 die**. This penalty applies once, regardless of overlapping smoke.

At the end of each round, roll a d8 for each marker. On **1–4**, it remains; on **5–8**, remove it.
