---
title: "EMP"
tags: ["keywords", "weapon-keywords"]
---

Deplete all personal Energy Shields carried by the target and every character within **4"** of it.

If the weapon targets a point, affect every character covered by its [[keywords/small-blast|Small Blast]] or [[keywords/large-blast|Large Blast]] template instead.

EMP does not automatically damage Constructs or disable other equipment unless an additional rule says so.
