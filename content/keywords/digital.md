---
title: "Digital"
tags: ["keywords", "character-keywords"]
---

You have no physical body. You may occupy a compatible storage device or attach to a friendly character with [[keywords/construct|Construct]] or [[keywords/neural-interface|Neural Interface]], called your host.

Attachment does not automatically grant control of the host or an additional activation. Your host’s movements carry you normally.

While unattached, you cannot participate in combat except through [[keywords/intrusion-protocol|Intrusion Protocol]], [[special-orders/hostile-takeover|Hostile Takeover]], or an ability that explicitly permits it. You may interact with accessible electronic systems outside combat through the normal RP rules.

Physical actions require an appropriate controlled body or platform.
