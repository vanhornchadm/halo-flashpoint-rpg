---
title: "Concussive"
tags: ["keywords", "weapon-keywords"]
---

*The impact leaves you reeling.*

When you score more successes than the target on an opposed Shoot or Assault attack, the target becomes Pinned in addition to suffering normal damage.

For an area attack, compare the attack against each affected character separately; apply Pinning to each character within 4" of the impact point whose opposed test you beat.

If the attack has no opposed test, Concussive does not create one.
