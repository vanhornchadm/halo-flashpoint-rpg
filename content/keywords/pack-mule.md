---
title: "Pack Mule"
tags: ["keywords", "equipment-and-integrated-systems"]
---

*There is always another attachment point.*

If the campaign uses inventory slots, you may carry up to **3 Items, 2 additional picked-up weapons, and 2 mission objects**.

You must still be physically capable of carrying them. This does not remove weapon-use restrictions.

If inventory slots are not used, Pack Mule establishes exceptional carrying capacity; the GM adjudicates the practical load.
