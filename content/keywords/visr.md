---
title: "VISR"
tags: ["keywords", "equipment-and-integrated-systems"]
---

*Your visor shows what ordinary sight misses.*

Gain **+1 die on AW tests**.

You may ignore the LOS restrictions and attack penalties caused by [[keywords/smoke|Smoke]] and [[keywords/active-camouflage|Active Camouflage]].

This does not reveal information beyond your sensors’ physical capabilities or allow sight through solid objects.
