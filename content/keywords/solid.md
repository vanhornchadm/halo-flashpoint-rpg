---
title: "Solid"
tags: ["keywords", "character-keywords"]
---

*You are not easily shifted.*

You cannot be Pinned or moved by weapon effects such as [[keywords/frag|Frag]] or [[keywords/knockback|Knockback]].

Other effects, including damage, apply normally. Solid does not prevent movement caused by collapsing terrain or other non-weapon causes unless another rule says otherwise.
