---
title: "Intrusion Protocol"
tags: ["keywords", "character-keywords"]
---

**Requirement:** [[keywords/digital|Digital]].

Spend one short action to target an enemy [[keywords/construct|Construct]] within 8" and LOS of your host or storage device. This is permitted while unattached or engaged.

Make a 3-dice Ranged test (X), using your RA, against the target's 3-dice Survive test (X). Apply no shooting modifiers. Excess successes cause Hits with AP 2. Headshots apply normally.

This attack cannot transfer you into or grant control of the target.
