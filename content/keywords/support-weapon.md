---
title: "Support Weapon"
tags: ["keywords", "weapon-keywords"]
---

While carrying this weapon:

- You cannot Sprint or throw grenades.
- Your Advance movement is capped at **4"**.
- You roll no dice when required to make a Fight test during an Assault.

You may still make an eligible Survive test.

If you move into base contact with an unattended Support Weapon and pick it up, that movement action immediately ends.
