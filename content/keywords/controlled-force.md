---
title: "Controlled Force"
tags: ["keywords", "talents"]
---

*You know when to stop.*

Before an Assault with an unarmed attack or suitable blunt weapon, declare an attempt to incapacitate.

If your attack reduces the target to zero HP, you may leave it alive and incapacitated. This does not prevent harm from other effects or subsequent attacks.

Weapons with [[keywords/lethal|Lethal]] or otherwise unsuitable for restraint cannot benefit.
