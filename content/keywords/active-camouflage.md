---
title: "Active Camouflage"
tags: ["keywords", "equipment-and-integrated-systems"]
---

While your personal Energy Shields are fully charged, enemies cannot draw LOS to you from more than **8"** away.

Enemies within 8" may draw LOS, but suffer **−2 dice** on Ranged tests against you.

While your shields are not fully charged, enemies may draw LOS normally but suffer **−1 die** on Ranged tests against you.

If you have no personal Energy Shields, use the −1 die effect. Barriers do not count as personal shields for this keyword.

Active Camouflage does not automatically make enemies unaware of your location.
