title: "Continuous Fire"
tags: ["keywords", "weapon-keywords"]
---

*Keep firing. Watch the heat.*

When making a Ranged test with this weapon, gain **[[keywords/weight-of-fire|Weight of Fire]] (2)**.

After the Shoot action resolves, make a **3-dice Survive test (2)**. On failure, suffer **1 wound**. Shields and armor do not prevent this damage.

The overheating test is required even if the attack fails.
