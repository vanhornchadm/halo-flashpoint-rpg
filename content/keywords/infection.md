*One wound can become an invasion.*

Constructs, [[keywords/digital|Digital]] characters, and Flood are immune to Infection.

When you cause at least one wound with a CC Assault attack, after damage prevention, you may attach to the target. Remove your model and give the target an **Infested marker**.

An attached Infection Form cannot activate or contribute to [[keywords/horde|Horde]]. A target may have only one Infested marker.

At the end of each subsequent activation of the host, it suffers **1 wound**, ignoring shields, armor, and damage reduction. The host retains control of its actions.

**Removal:** The host or an ally with physical access within 4" may spend a short Auxiliary action to remove the marker and destroy the infection. [[keywords/medic|Medic]] may remove it through medical treatment instead of healing. Healing HP alone does not remove it.

**Conversion:** Humans, Spartans, Jiralhanae, and Sangheili are eligible Combat Form hosts. An eligible Infested host reduced to zero HP converts at the end of the following round unless the infection is removed or its body is destroyed. This deadline cannot be accelerated.

An unattached Infection Form may instead take a long action within 4" of an intact, uninfected eligible casualty at zero HP to convert it immediately. Player characters cannot be converted during the round in which they first reach zero HP.

Replace the host and infection with a Flood Combat Form at its full **2 HP**, marked as activated. It cannot activate that round.

The Combat Form retains the host’s worn armor value and one weapon it can operate, but no Energy Shields, host Talents, or other armor benefits. Record any inherited attributes required by its profile. Conversion restores no ammunition or equipment uses.

Each body can be converted only once. A character with physical access within 4" may take a long action to destroy a casualty’s body, preventing conversion.
