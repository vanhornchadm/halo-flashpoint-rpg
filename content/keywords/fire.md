---
title: "Fire"
tags: ["keywords", "conditions-and-complications"]
---

*The flames demand attention.*

You may have only one Fire token. Additional Fire tokens are ignored while one is present.

During your activation, you may take a short Auxiliary action to remove it.

If the token remains at the end of your activation, suffer **1 wound**, then remove it.

Pinning and other restrictions still determine whether you can take the extinguishing action.
