title: "Repair"
tags: ["keywords", "character-keywords"]
---

*You can get it moving again.*

Once during your activation, spend one repair supply use and take a **short Auxiliary action** to restore **1 HP** to another operational friendly [[keywords/construct|Construct]] within 4" and LOS.

Suitable tools and physical or compatible system access are required. Each target may benefit only once per round.

Repair restores no shields, ammunition, or equipment uses. It cannot resurrect a destroyed [[keywords/construct|Construct]] and does not count as [[keywords/medic|Medic]].

Outside combat, routine work with compatible tools, parts, and time requires no roll. Each supply use restores 1 HP. Complications may require an INT test; relevant Skills apply.

Knight resurrection requires [[keywords/knight-reconstruction|Knight Reconstruction]].
