---
title: "Thrown (n)"
tags: ["keywords", "weapon-keywords", "equipment"]
---

This weapon or equipment is deployed by hand. **Thrown (n)** replaces a fixed range with a maximum throwing distance of **n + (4" × the thrower’s current STR)**, to a minimum of **4"**.

Always show the base distance in inches in the keyword, such as **Thrown (12")** or **Thrown (16")**. There is no default value: use the distance printed on the item’s profile. For example, **Thrown (16")** has a maximum distance of **16" + (4" × STR)**, minimum 4".

Use current STR, including functioning equipment bonuses. Negative STR reduces distance. Resolve inherited or unspecified STR before throwing. The thrower must be physically capable of handling and throwing the item.

| STR | Thrown (12") | Thrown (16") |
|---|---|---|
| −1 | 8" | 12" |
| 0 | 12" | 16" |
| +1 | 16" | 20" |
| +2 | 20" | 24" |

Use the item’s listed action, targeting requirements, placement procedure, and effects. Thrown determines distance only; it does not add a test, change attack dice, grant scatter, or change an effect’s radius. Equipment placed without a test still uses its normal placement procedure within Thrown range.

For an item with [[keywords/grenade|Grenade]], use Thrown to determine range, then follow the Grenade placement test and scatter rules. Scatter may carry the item beyond maximum throwing distance.

Recalculate distance using the new thrower’s STR whenever an item is thrown again, including with [[special-orders/return-to-sender|Return to Sender]]. Fired or projected items without Thrown retain their listed ranges.
