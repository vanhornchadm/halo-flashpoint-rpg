---
title: "Rapid Fire"
tags: ["keywords", "weapon-keywords"]
---

*Sometimes the objective is keeping heads down.*

When taking a Shoot action, choose normal fire or **Blaze Away** before rolling.

Blaze Away is a short action. Follow normal range and LOS requirements.

Roll a **4-dice Ranged test (X)** against the target’s **3-dice Survive test (X)**. Neither side benefits from Crouch, and [[keywords/optics|Optics]] provides no benefit.

If the attacker scores more successes, deplete one of the target’s personal shields, if available, and Pin the target. Otherwise, this effect does nothing.

Blaze Away causes no wounds. Armor and AP do not apply. Other applicable weapon effects still resolve unless they require Hits or wounds.
