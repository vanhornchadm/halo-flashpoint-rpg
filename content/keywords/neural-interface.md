---
title: "Neural Interface"
tags: ["keywords", "equipment-and-integrated-systems"]
---

You may host a friendly [[keywords/digital|Digital]] character.

While it is attached, whenever you use a stat, you may use its corresponding defined stat instead. An undefined or host-derived value does not create a new bonus or recursive substitution.

This does not transfer Talents, grant extra actions, or automatically give the AI control of your body.
