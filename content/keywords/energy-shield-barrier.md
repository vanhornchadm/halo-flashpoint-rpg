---
title: "Energy Shield Barrier (n)"
tags: ["keywords", "equipment-and-integrated-systems"]
---

*Portable cover made of energy.*

When deployed, place a barrier marker and **n charged shield tokens**.

The barrier protects every character within **4"** against ranged attacks originating outside that radius. It protects friendly and enemy characters alike.

It offers no protection against an attacker also within 4" of its marker.

Applicable barriers are depleted before personal shields. Multiple barriers may protect the same target, but each barrier has one shared pool of charges.

Remove a barrier when all its charges are depleted. Barriers do not regenerate.
