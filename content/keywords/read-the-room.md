title: "Read the Room"
tags: ["keywords", "talents"]
---

*You watch the people listening.*

After succeeding on an AW test to observe a social interaction, ask one additional question:

- Who appears most uneasy?
- Whose reaction are the others watching?
- What subject caused a noticeable change in behavior?

The GM answers from observable evidence. This does not reveal thoughts or prove dishonesty.
