---
title: "Scatter"
tags: ["keywords", "weapon-keywords"]
---

When an attack or effect scatters, mark its original target point and roll a **d8** on a flat part of the battlefield near that point.

Read the number on the uppermost face. The pointed tip of that face above the number indicates the scatter direction. Draw an imaginary line from the center of the die through that tip, then apply the same direction from the original target point.

Move the impact point a number of inches equal to the result on the die. For example, a result of **6** scatters the impact point **6"**.

Keep the die's rolled orientation until the direction has been agreed. If the die is cocked, leaves the battlefield, or its result or direction cannot be read clearly, reroll it.

After moving the impact point, apply any collision, bounce, or battlefield-edge rule specified by the attack or effect. Then center its [[keywords/small-blast|Small Blast]] or [[keywords/large-blast|Large Blast]] template on the final impact point.
