title: "Hostile Possession"
tags: ["keywords", "character-keywords"]
---

*Every unsecured machine is a possible doorway.*

**Requirement:** [[keywords/digital|Digital]].

Spend one short action to target an enemy [[keywords/construct|Construct]] within 8" and LOS of your host or storage device. This is permitted while unattached or engaged.

Choose one effect.

**Disable:** Make a 3-dice Ranged test (X), using your RA, against the target’s 3-dice Survive test (X). Apply no shooting modifiers. Excess successes cause Hits with AP 2. You do not transfer into the target.

**Hostile Takeover:** The target must have no attached [[keywords/digital|Digital]] character. It makes a 4-dice Survive test (1), losing one die for each wound already suffered, to a minimum of one die. Depleted shields do not count as wounds. No other dice modifiers apply.

On failure, detach from your current location and attach to the target, controlling its movement and actions. On success, nothing happens. Headshots apply normally to both effects.

When your [[keywords/neural-interface|Neural Interface]] host would make a CC attack against an eligible target, it may forgo that attack to allow a Hostile Takeover attempt. You must still spend your short action; this is not a free AI action. The target loses one additional Survive die, to a minimum of one.

Possession restores nothing and grants no additional activation or actions. Use the possessed host’s remaining activation allowance.

Possession ends when you detach, transfer elsewhere, the host is destroyed, or an applicable purge effect succeeds. Losing LOS alone does not end it.

Voluntary detachment costs a short action and requires an accessible legal destination.
