---
title: "Evade"
tags: ["keywords", "character-keywords"]
---

Once per round, after a Shoot action targeting you resolves, you may make an Advance move you are conscious, unpinned, and suffered no forced movement from that action.

This follows normal movement rules and may trigger an Assault.

## Evade (Reckless)

Use the normal Evade rule and its once-per-round limit, but you must finish the movement closer to the attacker that triggered it. Measure the distance to that attacker before and after moving.

If no legal move can finish closer, you cannot make this Evade move. This variant applies only when your profile explicitly grants **Evade (Reckless)**.
