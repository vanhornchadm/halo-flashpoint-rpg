Created using the built-in image generation tool from the previous Spartan card design. White writing areas, blank combat and RP values, outlined HP and equipment areas, and room for player identity and abilities. Raster design concept with no fixed print dimensions.

## Generation prompt

Edit the provided Halo Flashpoint RPG card into a BLANK player-fillable character card, optimized for handwriting with pencil or pen. Keep the same landscape proportions and familiar layout: identity upper right, combat stats mid-right, HP octagon to right, seven-column RP strip across middle-lower, weapons table underneath, slim side equipment rails, token tabs at edges. Produce one flat front-facing high-resolution card graphic, no perspective, no props, tiny exterior margin. Preserve the teal sci-fi visual language, condensed bold labels, clipped panel corners, and rounded outer corners, but remove all distressed textures and make all writable surfaces PURE WHITE with fine medium-gray outlines. Teal only on small headers, labels, outer accents. Black or dark teal text. Practical generous empty spaces are the priority.
Remove the Spartan illustration and UNSC logo so this works for any player character or species. Replace the left artwork area with a large white panel labeled "CHARACTER / EMBLEM" in small text at top with ample blank space to draw or paste a portrait. Beneath that in the left upper area include three spaced handwriting lines labeled "Species", "Background", "Faction".
Replace big title with an exceptionally broad WHITE empty name field, a small dark teal header label "CHARACTER NAME". Under that add "Talents / Abilities" with two generously spaced blank handwriting lines. No preset character name, no Spartan text, no preset traits.
Combat cells keep headers "RA", "FI", "SV" in teal header caps, with large blank white boxes beneath. Movement chevron retain outline shape, small "SPD" label and white empty center, no printed slash or values. Armour shield retain outline shape, small "AR" label and white empty center. HP octagon white inside with thin outline, small "HP" top label, empty center for wound tokens and a small separate blank white bottom compartment labeled "MAX". No preset numbers.
Full-width "RP ATTRIBUTES" slim teal section bar, followed by seven equal columns with teal header caps and tall entirely blank white write-in cells. EXACT order: "STR", "AGL", "INT", "AW", "WIL", "FEL", "LCK". Each header appears exactly once. No numbers, plus signs, zeros, or example values in any cell. Remove "Equipped values • MJOLNIR bonuses included".
Weapon table headers EXACTLY: "Weapon Type", "Weapon", "Range", "AP", "Keywords". Two generously tall rows with WHITE backgrounds; first row type only "Close Combat", second row type only "Ranged"; all other cells completely blank for handwriting. Give Weapon and Keywords columns generous widths. Thin gray gridlines, no shaded data rows.
Equipment edge rails retain light outline and small dark vertical labels "Close combat weapon slot" and "Pick up slot" with white interior. Top four shield token tabs and bottom four item tabs should be white outlined shapes. Discreet footer text "UNOFFICIAL RPG CHARACTER CARD".
Do not fill any writable field. No instructional sample handwriting, no placeholder dashes, no watermark, no gradients, no paper texture. Make the design clean, ink economical, and easy to write on while recognizably matching the supplied card.
